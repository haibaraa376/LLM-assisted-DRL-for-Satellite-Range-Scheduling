A B S T R A C T
Satellite range scheduling is an important issue in the field of satellite mission planning, which greatly affects the development of satellite industry. This paper analyzed satellite range scheduling problem, constructed a multiobjective SRSP (MO-SRSP) model and proposed an improved multi-objective evolutionary algorithm (MOEA), called learning-guided nondominated sorting genetic algorithm II (LGNSGAII) that contains a learning mechanism. Learning mechanisms can speed up optimization process. Meanwhile, another algorithm called task-time window selection algorithm (TTSA) is also proposed. Specifically, it can select satellite ground stations time windows for tasks. TTSA includes three location selection methods and two location movement methods, both are used to select the appropriate execution location for tasks. Experiments show that the proposed algorithm can solve MO-SRSP better than several comparison algorithms. In other words, this algorithm we proposed has a broad practical application prospect.
1. Introduction
Satellites meet military and civilian mission requirements by providing a variety of services, such as environmental monitoring, disaster prediction, satellite navigation. Daily service of satellites relies on the communication between satellite ground stations and satellites.
This communication process with satellite ground station is called satellite range, or satellite measurement and control. In this paper, an optimization problem, which is also known as Satellite TT&C scheduling problem, is named satellite range scheduling problem. In SRSP, the goal that match many satellite resources with measurement and control resources, and select appropriate measurement and control tasks to complete, is the key to ensure normal operation and successful completion of various missions. Using satellite resources efficiently is of great significance for the development of satellite industry and for humanity.
SRSP is to select appropriate location and time for satellites’ communication with ground stations. More generally, it needs to determine the time for satellites communicating with ground stations.
Communication between satellite and station must be carried out at a position where two antennas are visible to each other. This time interval is called ground station visible time window. Satellite measurement and control tasks must be done within the range of visible time windows of ground stations. Numerous measurement and control requirements combined with the limited resources of ground stations make SRSP hard to solve, thus followed by oversubscribed scheduling problem. Meanwhile, SRSP has limitations on technology and equipment. These constraints increase the complexity of this problem. Although this problem can be solved by an exact solution algorithm in the case of small-scale tasks, it is difficult to obtain a feasible solution in a limited time once the task scale is increased. In contrast, evolutionary algorithms are more suitable to solve SRSP.
Satellite range scheduling problem has always been an important focus in the field of satellite mission planning. This problem is similar to vehicle routing problem with time windows (VRPTW) [31]. However,
MO-SRSP will cause tasks to fail due to high resources of the mission relative to ground stations. And there is no problem of vehicle movement in this problem, but the choice of time window resources. Due to different backgrounds of problem, our problem and model are more complicated than VRPTW problem. Dynamic optimization process of some complex systems is also useful for reference [24,25]. This problem has two major characteristics, one is oversubscribed scheduling, the other is that the problem belongs to a NP-Hard problem, and these two points have been confirmed by the research [1,2]. edu/sched/data.html. This data set is rarely used in research today because the scale of tasks is small, and the simplification of data set for actual scenes has little guiding significance for the application.
Methods used by the researchers can be divided into exact solution algorithms and approximate solution algorithms. As measurement and control tasks increase rapidly under conditions of increasing measurement and control resources, research on exact solution algorithms in recent years is rare. An important reason is that the algorithm fails to achieve good results in a short time in a large-scale task scenario.
Approximate solution algorithm can be mainly divided into three categories, a heuristic algorithm using rules, evolutionary optimization algorithm and the above two hybrid algorithms. Hybrid algorithms take into account advantages of the first two algorithms and overcomes shortcomings of using an algorithm alone, which can achieve good planning results in large-scale task scenarios.

2. Problem definition and mathematical model
2.1. Description of SRSP
Satellite ground station completes the process of monitoring satellites’ status through measurement and control and uploads mission instructions for satellites. Satellite range scheduling allows satellites to operate well in orbit and complete mission requests. Firstly, we take two satellites and one satellite ground station as an example. One of the two satellites is a low-orbit satellite and the other is a high-orbit satellite.
Each of the two satellites needs to complete a measurement and control task. As shown in Fig. 1, the low-orbit satellite has a shorter time window due to the lower altitude of flight orbit, while the available time window for the high-orbit satellite measurement and control tasks is longer. When
LEO1 and GEO1 in the figure pass through the same satellite ground station one by one, it is necessary to select an appropriate execution time for measurement and control tasks of these two satellites, so that both measurement and control tasks can be performed as successful as possible.
During a certain measurement and control cycle, there are many available time windows for ground stations that can be used for measurement and control opportunities. Each measurement and control task request must be made within the visible time window of the ground station. When a task occupies part or all of a ground station time window position, other tasks will no longer be able to select this time window position. At the same time, since measurement and control tasks need to be carried out within a certain time range, the position of the ground station time window available for each measurement and control task is limited. In addition, the ground station antenna needs to match with the satellite antenna to execute the measurement and control tasks. That is to say, the ground station needs to rotate the antenna to a certain angle after performing a task before the next measurement and control task can be performed.

Multi-objective SRSP has two decision variables. One is xi;j, indicating whether the j-th task of the i-th satellite is executed. If the measurement and control task can be performed, xi;j is 1, otherwise, xi;j is 0. Another decision variable is si;j, which indicates the start execution time of the j-th task of the i-th satellite. After deciding the execution of each measurement and control task and the time of the task, an execution plan of task sequence can be obtained, and specific execution planning of each task in the measurement and control task set is included in the solution.
In order to transform SRSP problem from a complex engineering problem into a scientific problem that facilitates the construction of mathematical models. The following assumptions are required:
1. Only consider satellite measurement and control problems, other imaging observation and data downlink processes will not affect the measurement and control;
2. All devices are in normal operation, and the failure of the measurement and control tasks caused by factors such as equipment operation failures is not considered;
3. Once a measurement and control task begins, it will not be allowed to end halfway;
4. After a task starts, it cannot be preempted by other tasks;
5. Ignoring the time required for satellite and satellite ground stations to construct communication links, only considering the planning and scheduling process;
6. Satellite power can support the needs of measurement and control tasks;
7. Rotation of the antenna requires a certain conversion time, which cannot be ignored.
Multi-objective SRSP (MO-SRSP) reference the literature [14,15,21] optimization objectives, mainly include two optimization objectives: (1) overall profit of the task sequence; (2) task failure rate in the task sequence. For the first optimization goal, the maximum profit of the task sequence should be achieved. For the second optimization goal, if it has the fewer failed tasks in the task sequence, the smaller the task failure rates it will be. 3. Method
To solve multi-objective SRSP (MO-SRSP), we gave two algorithms, one is learning-guided NSGA-II (LGNSGAII) and the other is task-time window selection algorithm (TTSA). LGNSGAII is used to continuously generate new solutions and improve the performance of task scheduling.
TTSA is used to determine whether a task is executed, and the execution time of the task. LGNSGAII will be introduced in Section 3.1. After that, an introduction to TTSA will be given in Section 3.2.
3.1. Learning-guided NSGA-II (LGNSGAII)
3.1.1. Overall flow of the algorithm
Learning-guided NSGA-II (LGNSGAII) is based on the classical NSGAII algorithm in multi-objective optimization algorithm and is adapted to multi-objective SRSP (MO-SRSP) [33,35]. Learning mechanism is introduced so that the algorithm can be optimized selectively. LGNSGAII is first initialized to get the initial population. Evolutionary search can optimize population and produce offspring population, which is called genetic operation, including selection, crossover, mutation, and so on.
The number of individuals within the population determined whether the elite individuals in the population are selected by non-dominated sorting and crowding distance sorting, and individual size of population is fixed at a stable number. An evolutionary search is then performed to obtain a new generation of populations that are merged with the previous generation's population for a new cyclical process. Flow chart of
LGNSGAII is shown in Fig. 2.
Before genetic operation, learning mechanism obtains the conflict rate of task sequence, and automatically adjusts the weight of conflict rate by using individual's performance. Both learning mechanism and selection mechanism correspond to an individual. It can improve the task selection of genetic operation a lot. In this way, the optimization process can be made to have a definite purpose, so that the task sequence proceeds in the direction in which the task conflict is reduced. The learning mechanism has a complexity of OðnÞ.
In order to adjust to the learning mechanism and problem, we gave two crossover operators and two mutation operators. These operators are matched to MO-SRSP. Crossover and mutation make task sequence not fall into local optimum. Key steps in the specific LGNSGAII, genetic operation and learning mechanisms will be described in the following sections. The pseudo code of LGNSGAII is shown in Table of the algorithm.

3.1.3. Initial population generation
The initial solution is generated by a randomly generated method, and the length of each solution is equal to the length of the task sequence.
Each measurement task is allocated to a location where task scheduling is performed in a random manner. In this way, an initial planning scheme is obtained, and the subsequent optimization process is performed after the sequence is initialized.
3.1.4. Non-dominated sorting
Non-dominated sorting method reference the [20] proposed a fast non-dominated sorting method. It is a sorting algorithm with complexity
Oðmn2Þ, where m represents the number of objectives and n represents the number of individuals in the population. The non-dominated sorting first determines the dominating number to be the first layer according to the dominance number of each solution in the entire solution space. Then it selects an individual from the first layer and traverses its corresponding dominating set for each individual the dominance number is subtracted by 1. If dominance number becomes 0, the second layer is obtained. By this way, we can get all layers. When comparing different results, we prefer to a solution with a lower rank.

Experimental environment: The proposed algorithms are implemented by Matlab2017a on a desktop with Core I7-7700 3.6 GHz CPU,
8 GB memory, and Windows 7 operating system. Experimental examples generated by STK are generated based on actual satellite application scenarios.
Examples in this experimental part of this paper are close to the real application scenarios. Taking measurement and control tasks within a scheduling period as the planning goal, one of our cycles here is set to one day, which is 24 h. In one day's time, we constructed test cases for multiple satellites and multiple ground stations. All measurement and control tasks are generated according to the actual satellite operation. Satellites conclude two types: low-orbit satellites and high-orbit satellites. Satellites and ground stations in the experiment are all from China, and ground stations are only set up in China. In this paper, different task scales are set for different types of measurement and control tasks to test the performance of proposed

Comparison algorithms in this paper chooses four multi-objective optimization algorithms. We selected NSGAII algorithm, MOEA/D algorithm, MOEA/DD algorithm and MOMA/D algorithm respectively. Genetic algorithms have a great performance in SRSP. NSGAII algorithm and MOEA/D algorithm are optimum optimization methods in multiobjective optimization algorithm [24,25]. MOEA/DD algorithm and
MOMA/D algorithm are multi-objective evolutionary algorithms with very good optimization results in two recent studies, which are state-of-the-art algorithms. The following is a brief introduction to
MOEA/D algorithm, MOEA/DD algorithm and MOMA/D algorithm.
MOEA/D algorithm: Multi-objective evolutionary algorithm based on decomposition (MOEA/D) is an optimization algorithm for solving multiobjectives proposed by Zhang in 2007 [22]. The core idea of this algorithm is to transform a multi-objective optimization problem into a single-objective optimization problem and perform simultaneous optimization. This is a method that is sufficiently classic and optimized in multi-objective optimization.
MOMA/D algorithm: It is a new algorithm proposed by Zhang [32], named as multi-objective memetic algorithm based on decomposition for big optimization problems (MOMA/D), is proposed. It is a new improved memetic algorithm.
MOEA/DD algorithm: Deb [34] suggests a unified paradigm, which combines dominance and decomposition-based approaches, for many-objective optimization. Our major purpose is to exploit the merits from both of dominance- and decomposition-based approaches to balance the convergence and diversity of the evolutionary process. It has

5. Conclusion
Based on satellite measurement and control process, this paper proposes a multi-objective SRSP (MO-SRSP) mathematical model. The objectives is to maximize task sequence profit and minimize task failure rate. We also give some constraints. After analyzing characteristics, we propose a learning mechanism with a new index called task conflict rate based on NSGAII algorithm. This learning mechanism can guide the optimization process of evolutionary algorithms.
We have designed various types of experiments to verify effectiveness of the proposed algorithm. It can be concluded from experimental result that our algorithm is more ideal for low-orbit satellite and high-orbit satellite range scheduling, which results a better optimization effect and calculation speed. LGNSGAII has outstanding performance for complex application scenarios, especially in large-scale application scenarios. We also carried out measurement and control scheduling for integrated tasks. Compared with classic MOMA/D algorithm, LGNSGAII is more suitable for solving MO-SRSP.
In the following research, we will consider MO-SPSR problem with more objective functions and give a high-efficiency solving algorithm for high-dimensional optimization problems. Besides, how to get a more evenly distributed Pareto front can be chosen as a research emphasis in the future.
